GOOD TO KNOW

Clear terms. Better service.

We want ordering to feel simple and transparent.

Where do you deliver?

We currently focus on RR Nagar, Bengaluru. Please share your apartment or location so we can confirm coverage.

How does payment work?

The product amount is paid to the shop, plus the ₹19 delivery charge. For orders above ₹500, a small advance may be requested before purchase; the advance amount and payment method will be confirmed with you first.

Are all items guaranteed to be available?

No. Items, prices and delivery times depend on local shop stock and delivery capacity. We confirm substitutions or changes with you before proceeding.

Can I order medicines?

Eligible pharmacy items are subject to applicable law, valid prescriptions where required, and fulfilment by a licensed pharmacy. We cannot supply restricted medicines unlawfully or offer medical advice.

When is my order confirmed?

Submitting the WhatsApp message is a request, not confirmation. We will reply to confirm item availability, final prices, payment and estimated delivery.
